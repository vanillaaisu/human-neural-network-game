How to play

THE RELAY / 4 STEPS

Make a line. Pass the signal. No peeking backwards.

1Input

The first person gets a secret scene. Give them 10 seconds to study it.

2Pass it on

They describe it to the next person, who draws what they hear.

3Repeat

Keep alternating: drawing, describing, drawing. No talking twice in a row.

4Output

The last person guesses the original. Reveal the scene and compare the chain.

✳

The AI connection

Each person is a layer in a model. Notice what gets amplified, changed, or lost as information passes through.

Facilitator’s corner

ROUND 01

Show the secret scene to the first person only. Keep the drawings hidden until the final guess.
